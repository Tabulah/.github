## Hello. We are Tabulah Education.

We believe education should work for all children, parents, and teachers alike.

No child should be left behind because the school curriculum doesn't fit them, but no teacher should need to suffer the consequences of a student's actions.

We attempt to bridge the best of both worlds. A learning system in which children might actually enjoy to learn, but _never_ at the expense of a teacher's sanity.

There are many children who simply do not function properly in a classroom with 30 people in it. And that's okay. It is our responsibility, as the adults, to find a way for the next generation to learn and **enjoy learning.**

_**No child should suffer because the system wasn't built for them, and no teacher should burn out trying to make it work.**_
